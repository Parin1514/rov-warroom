# Design

Abyssal Rift is the setting, not the navigation model. A narrow battle banner uses publisher art with a slow depth drift and a reduced-motion fallback. Below it, dark mineral surfaces keep drafting controls still. Blue and coral identify sides. Amber identifies the active draft slot and primary action.

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Canvas | #10141d | Page ground |

Use Noto Sans Thai with a system sans fallback, tabular numerals for timers, and Lucide strokes for icons. Controls remain conventional and keyboard operable.
